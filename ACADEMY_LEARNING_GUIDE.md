# Cube Hero Academy — Expanded Learning Guide

## What changed
- Full standard case catalogue: **41 F2L + 57 OLL + 21 PLL = 119** verified, distinct 3×3 training cases.
- **Cross** is intuitive, so there is no finite universal Cross algorithm table. The Academy instead includes 12 practice move patterns and a Cross Coach that computes a verified Cross from a random 3×3 scramble.
- 3 additional 2-look OLL edge drills, basic triggers, advanced case search, a prepared pattern diagram, and a real turning-cube player for every case.
- Case favorites, **Mark as learned**, simple recognition advice, progress saved in the browser.
- More detailed beginner instructions and common mistakes for Cross, F2L, OLL, and PLL.

## How to study
1. Start with **Notation Lab**. Understand R/L/U/D/F/B, prime ('), double (2), and wide/slab moves in advanced formulas.
2. Study **Cross**. Match all 4 white cross edges and their side centers. Use Cross Coach to generate a fresh scramble and view a verified answer when you get stuck.
3. Learn **intuitive F2L**. Pair corners and edges, starting with free pairs; 41 reference cases are available.
4. Learn **2-Look OLL** before full OLL. Use the starter filter to practice edge orientation plus the seven basic corner cases.
5. Learn **2-Look PLL** before all 21 PLL cases. The starter list includes A and edge-permutation patterns.
6. After you can finish a cube consistently, move to **full OLL/PLL**, finger tricks, and timed racing.

## Important conventions
- Cross assumes white bottom, yellow top and side colors lined up with the centers.
- The 119 example cube setups are generated by applying the inverse of the chosen algorithm to a solved cube. The animation is a real physical-layer sequence for that prepared state, **not** a claim that one formula solves every scramble.
- Some F2L reference sequences are best learned in their own slot order, not after every other F2L pair is already solved. Watch which pieces each sequence moves.
- F2L cases refer to the **front-right** slot. Mirrors and cube orientation changes let you apply those ideas in other slots.
- To inspect a last-layer case, view the top face **and** its side stickers. A single top silhouette may not be enough.
- Whole cube rotations and lowercase wide turns are supported in the Academy's physical 3D renderer.
- Cross Coach uses the same verified CFOP worker as racing. A new random scramble may take time to compute on slower computers.

## Progress & safety
Learning records stay in browser-local data and are separate from AI/Ghost race statistics. Existing favorites from the first Academy version are migrated to the corresponding full case IDs when possible. The race and inspection engine files are not changed.
